Understanding the Analysis

📊

Returns

Returns measure the percentage change in price over time. Simple returns show direct percentage changes, while log returns are mathematically additive and preferred in quantitative finance for statistical analysis and modeling.

📈

Volatility

Volatility represents the degree of price variation over time, measured as the standard deviation of returns. Higher volatility indicates larger price swings and greater uncertainty. We use rolling windows to capture recent market behavior.

🔍

Market Regimes

Market regimes are periods where behavior (returns + volatility) exhibits similar patterns. Using K-Means clustering on standardized features, we identify distinct behavioral states without predefined labels—a core unsupervised learning technique.

⚠️

No Predictions

This application performs descriptive analysis only. It identifies patterns and calculates statistical metrics from historical data. It does not predict future prices, recommend trades, or provide investment advice. All analysis is retrospective.
