Understanding the Metrics

📉 Returns

Returns measure the percentage change in price over time. Simple returns show direct percentage changes, while log returns are mathematically additive and preferred in quantitative finance for statistical analysis.

📊 Volatility

Volatility represents the degree of price variation over time. Higher volatility indicates larger price swings and greater uncertainty. We use rolling windows to capture recent market behavior rather than historical averages.

⚠️ No Predictions

This application performs descriptive analysis only. It identifies patterns and calculates statistical metrics from historical data. It does not predict future prices, recommend trades, or provide investment advice.

Explainability

This system avoids prediction and instead focuses on interpretable signals. Returns describe movement, volatility describes risk, and clustering groups similar behavioral periods without labels. Every metric is traceable to its calculation, and every decision is based on transparent statistical methods. This approach prioritizes understanding over black-box predictions, making the analysis reliable and trustworthy for exploratory purposes.
