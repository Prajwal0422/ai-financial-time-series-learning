Analysis Interpretation

Statistical Returns

We use logarithmic returns for modeling because they are time-additive. This allows us to treat market movements as a continuous statistical signal rather than discrete price points, enabling more sophisticated mathematical analysis.

Volatility Clustering

Market volatility exhibits clustering behavior - periods of high volatility tend to group together. Our analysis detects these regime shifts to understand market state transitions and risk dynamics.

Pattern Recognition

We employ unsupervised machine learning to identify patterns in high-dimensional feature space, removing human bias from market behavior analysis and discovering natural groupings in data.

Analysis Scope

This system focuses on historical pattern analysis and statistical interpretation. We do not provide predictions or trading signals - our goal is understanding market behavior through data science.
