Statistical Returns

We use logarithmic returns for modeling because they are time-additive. This allows us to treat market movements as a continuous statistical signal rather than just price points.

Volatility Clusters

Market volatility is rarely random; it often appears in clusters. Our system detects these shifts in regime to understand when the market is transitioning between stable and stressed states.

Pattern Recognition

Instead of manual chart reading, we use unsupervised ML to find clusters in high-dimensional feature space. This removes human bias from the analysis of market behavior.
